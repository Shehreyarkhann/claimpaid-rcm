# ClaimPaid — Privacy Policy

**Version:** v1-draft — **DRAFT pending attorney review.** Last updated: 2026-10-23

ClaimPaid ("we," "us"), operated by Shehreyar Khan, Bay Shore, NY, operates claimpaid.io and the ClaimPaid RCM and ClaimPaid AI services (the "Services"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. Contact: support@claimpaid.io.

## 1. Our role: controller vs. processor

- **Account, billing, and usage data:** we act as the data *controller* — we decide how it's used.
- **Data you upload** (claims, payer policies, documents, patient information): you are the controller; we act as your *processor* (and, where PHI is involved, your *business associate* under a signed BAA). We process it only to provide the Services.

## 2. What we collect

**Account data:** name, email, company name, role — provided at signup or by your admin.

**Billing data:** plan and payment status. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never receive or store full card numbers.

**Usage data:** login events, feature usage, error logs, IP address, browser/device type — used for security, reliability, and product improvement.

**Support communications:** emails and messages you send to support@claimpaid.io.

**Customer data (including PHI):** whatever you upload — claims, denials, payer documents, eligibility records. This is yours; see §5.

**What we do NOT collect:** we don't run ads, we don't sell personal data, and we don't buy marketing lists.

## 3. Sub-processors

We use a small set of vendors, each handling only what's necessary:

| Vendor | Purpose | Data handled |
|---|---|---|
| DigitalOcean (droplet, NYC region) | Hosting | All service data, at rest on the VPS disk |
| Stripe | Payment processing | Billing identifiers; card data goes to Stripe directly |
| Resend | Transactional email (MFA codes, notifications) | Recipient email addresses, message content |

Current sub-processor list is maintained here; we will notify customers before adding a new one that touches customer data.

## 4. How we use data

- Provide, secure, and improve the Services
- Bill your subscription and send transactional emails
- Respond to support requests
- Meet legal obligations (e.g., tax records)

**AI training:** We do not use your uploaded customer data to train machine-learning models for other customers. ClaimPaid AI answers from *your* configured knowledge base only.

## 5. PHI

When your uploaded data includes protected health information, our handling is governed by the BAA you sign with us (required before uploading PHI and before activating a paid subscription — see Terms §6). Sign it electronically in the app under Settings → Compliance. Without a signed BAA, do not upload PHI.

## 6. Security

Encrypted transport (TLS) in transit; access controls and authentication (including optional MFA); audit logging of data access; daily backups with 7-day rotation. We apply security updates to the host promptly. No method is 100% secure — if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you without unreasonable delay and no later than required by law.

## 7. Retention

- **Account and billing records:** kept for the life of the account plus 7 years (tax/legal).
- **Usage and error logs:** up to 24 months, then aggregated or deleted.
- **Customer data:** retained while your subscription is active; deleted within 90 days of termination unless you request export/earlier deletion or the law requires retention.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your state (including NY and California residents), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and opt out of any sale or sharing (we don't sell). Email support@claimpaid.io; we respond within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise rights over data your *employer* uploaded (e.g., as an end user), contact your organization's admin first.

## 9. Cookies

We use only functional cookies/session storage required for login and app function. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 10. Children

The Services are business tools, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## 11. Changes

Material changes take effect 30 days after notice (email or in-app). Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions or rights requests: support@claimpaid.io, ClaimPaid, operated by Shehreyar Khan, Bay Shore, NY.
