**BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT**

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of electronic signature recorded below ("Effective Date") by and between:

- **Covered Entity / Customer:** the organization identified in the electronic signature record below ("Customer")
- **Business Associate:** ClaimPaid, operated by Shehreyar Khan, Bay Shore, NY ("ClaimPaid")

(Each a "Party," together the "Parties.")

**RECITALS.** Customer uses ClaimPaid's revenue-cycle software services (the "Services"), available at claimpaid.io, which involve the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission of Protected Health Information ("PHI") on Customer's behalf. The Parties wish to comply with the HIPAA Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules (45 CFR Parts 160, 164) and the HITECH Act.

## 1. Definitions

Terms used but not defined here have the meanings in HIPAA/HITECH: *Breach, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Electronic PHI (ePHI), Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured PHI,* and *Use*.

## 2. Obligations of ClaimPaid (Business Associate)

ClaimPaid agrees to:

a) **Use and disclose PHI only as permitted.** Use or disclose PHI only as necessary to provide the Services, or as required by law. No other use or disclosure without Customer's written authorization.

b) **Minimum necessary.** Limit uses, disclosures, and requests to the minimum necessary to accomplish the purpose.

c) **Safeguards.** Implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect ePHI, as required by the Security Rule — including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, authentication, and audit logging.

d) **Mitigation.** Mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect of an impermissible use or disclosure of which it becomes aware.

e) **Breach and incident reporting.** Report to Customer, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than **10 business days** after discovery: (i) any use or disclosure not permitted by this Agreement; (ii) any Security Incident; and (iii) any Breach of Unsecured PHI, with the information required by 45 CFR §164.402 to enable Customer to meet its notification duties. Reports go to Customer's designated contact and to support@claimpaid.io.

f) **Subcontractors.** Ensure any subcontractor or sub-processor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on ClaimPaid's behalf agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions. Current sub-processors touching PHI: DigitalOcean (hosting). ClaimPaid will notify Customer before adding another.

g) **Individual rights.** Within 10 business days of Customer's request: make PHI in a Designated Record Set available for access (45 CFR §164.524); make PHI available for amendment (§164.526); and provide an accounting of disclosures (§164.528) as required.

h) **HHS access.** Make internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary for compliance determination.

i) **No PHI for AI training.** ClaimPaid will not use Customer's PHI to train machine-learning models for any other customer or purpose.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures by ClaimPaid

a) As necessary to perform the Services described in the applicable order/subscription.
b) For ClaimPaid's proper management and administration, or to carry out its legal responsibilities — provided any disclosure is required by law or made to a recipient who agrees in writing to protect the PHI.
c) To provide Data Aggregation services relating to Customer's Health Care Operations, if applicable.
d) De-identified data: PHI that has been de-identified in accordance with 45 CFR §164.514 is no longer PHI and is not subject to this Agreement.

## 4. Obligations of Customer

a) Customer will not request ClaimPaid to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate HIPAA if done by Customer.
b) Customer will notify ClaimPaid of any restriction on use/disclosure in its notice of privacy practices, or any change in/revocation of an Individual's permission, to the extent it affects ClaimPaid's handling of PHI.
c) Customer is responsible for obtaining all consents and authorizations required for ClaimPaid to process PHI.
d) Customer will not upload PHI to the Services until this Agreement is signed.

## 5. Term and termination

a) This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and terminates when all PHI is destroyed or returned, or when the underlying Services agreement terminates.
b) Either Party may terminate for material breach with 30 days' written notice and opportunity to cure; if cure is not feasible, termination is immediate.
c) **Effect of termination.** Upon termination, ClaimPaid will, at Customer's election, return or securely destroy all PHI (including copies held by subcontractors) and retain no copies — except where retention is required by law, in which case protections of this Agreement continue indefinitely for retained PHI.

## 6. Miscellaneous

a) **Regulatory references.** Any reference to HIPAA/HITECH refers to the law as amended; if the law changes to require an amendment here, the Parties will negotiate in good faith.
b) **Interpretation.** Ambiguities are resolved to permit compliance with HIPAA/HITECH.
c) **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing here confers rights on any person other than the Parties.
d) **Governing law.** State of New York.
e) **Entire agreement.** This Agreement plus the underlying Services agreement constitute the entire agreement on PHI handling; in case of conflict on PHI matters, this Agreement controls.

**Electronic signature.** By signing electronically in the ClaimPaid app, the signatory represents they are authorized to bind Customer. The signature record (signer name, title, email, timestamp, IP address, and SHA-256 hash of this text) is stored by ClaimPaid as the executed counterpart of this Agreement.
