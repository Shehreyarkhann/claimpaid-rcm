FAQ

Questions billing teams
actually ask.

What does ClaimPaid cost?

RCM Starter is $199/month, RCM Pro + AI is $499/month, and ClaimPaid AI standalone is $99/month. Enterprise is custom. Every paid plan starts with a 30-day free trial — no credit card required.

Is the 30-day free trial really free — no credit card?

Yes. Selecting a plan starts a real 30-day trial of the app — no credit card required. A guided 5-step onboarding wizard sets up your workspace, and you can sign in with Google. Trial days remaining sync to billing, so checkout only starts paid billing after the trial ends.

Can I sign a BAA with ClaimPaid?

Yes — ClaimPaid includes built-in BAA e-signing, so the business associate agreement is executed inside the platform before any PHI flows. Do billing companies need a BAA? →

How do eligibility checks work?

ClaimPaid runs eligibility checks inside the workspace, configured per organization — or ask the agent to check any claim before it goes out. Results are stored on the claim, so CO-50 non-covered-service denials get stopped before submission, not after.

Which EHR integrations does ClaimPaid support?

ClaimPaid connects to athenahealth via OAuth2 to sync patient and claim data. Other EHR integrations are evaluated per organization.

Where does my data go when I use ClaimPaid's AI features?

The agent works inside your ClaimPaid workspace, using a frontier AI model via API to read your queue, draft, and answer. Three mechanics protect you: nothing the agent proposes is written without a human approving it, every agent action and decision is recorded in your audit log, and your workspace's data trains only your workspace's predictions — it is never pooled with other customers' data. We make no HIPAA certification claims: no such certification exists.

Is my data used to train models for other customers?

No. Predictions and appeal-angle learning are computed per workspace, from that workspace's own decided appeals and posted remittances only. Your data never trains another customer's predictions or answers.

Does the AI send anything to payers?

No. The agent drafts and prepares — appeal letters, code sets, exception resolutions — and a person on your team approves each one and submits it through your current path. Nothing is transmitted to a payer by the AI.

What does the agent do each morning?

It opens the day with a brief: what changed while you were away, a ranked work plan (deadline pressure times dollars at stake), and appeal drafts already written for the claims that need them — waiting for your approval. A watchdog digest emails the same picture when you're away from the app.

Is my data encrypted?

Database backups are encrypted with age (X25519) the moment they're written, and each backup is decrypt-verified before its plaintext is deleted. All traffic uses TLS. We make no HIPAA certification claims: no such certification exists.

Does ClaimPaid write appeal letters automatically?

The agent drafts payer-specific appeal letters from the denial reason code — using the letter engine's categories (timely filing, medical necessity, authorization, coding, eligibility, duplicate, bundling, experimental), your letterhead, and, once your workspace has decided appeals, the argument angles that have actually won against that payer. Every draft waits for a human to approve it; approving saves the appeal, and your team submits it. The coding assistant suggests codes from a 370-code library only when the documentation supports them — unverified codes are never applied on the AI's say-so.

Which payers' timely-filing deadlines does ClaimPaid track?

Seven major payers out of the box: Aetna (120 days), Cigna (180), UnitedHealthcare (90), Anthem (180), Medicare (365), NY Medicaid (90), and Humana (180) — with custom payers and deadlines configurable per organization. Related guides: timely-filing limits by payer, appealing an Aetna timely-filing denial, Medicare's timely-filing deadline.

Can I use ClaimPaid AI without the RCM platform?

Yes. ClaimPaid AI is available as a standalone $99/month plan at claimpaid.io/ai/ — cited answers from the Payer Policy Handbook, the quote-verified coding assistant, and cited answers over your own documents (policies, contracts, manuals). RCM Pro customers get it included with one shared login.

How long does implementation take?

Most teams are live within 24 hours — a 5-step onboarding wizard gets your workspace set up on a real 30-day trial, no implementation project required. Support is handled through a two-way helpdesk with a 24-hour response SLA.

When is ClaimPaid not the right fit?

ClaimPaid is software, not a billing service — if you want a company to do the billing work for you rather than software for your team, we're not that. It's web-based SaaS (not on-premise), and its payer content is built around US billing workflows.
