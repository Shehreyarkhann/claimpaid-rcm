CO-24: Covered under capitation or a managed care plan

Group CO — Contractual Obligation · Denial Code Library · Updated October 29, 2026

What CO-24 means

Charges are covered under a capitation agreement or managed care plan. The service is paid through a fixed per-member arrangement rather than fee-for-service, so the payer will not pay the claim line separately.

What it usually means on a real remittance

CO-24 appears in managed care and delegated-model markets. It usually means the claim went to the wrong pocket: the service is included in capitation paid to your group or to a delegated entity, or it belongs to a carve-out arrangement.

Common causes

  • The patient is enrolled in a capitated plan or delegated group that pays your organization a fixed amount for this category of care
  • The service belongs to a carve-out vendor (lab, radiology, behavioral health) under the plan's managed care arrangement
  • The patient was assigned to a different primary care or risk group for that date of service
  • The claim was sent to the health plan when it should have gone to the delegated medical group or IPA

How to fix or appeal CO-24

  1. Confirm the arrangement: check the eligibility response and your contract's division of financial responsibility for this service and date
  2. If the claim belongs to a delegated group or carve-out vendor, reroute it there — do not appeal to the plan
  3. If your organization holds the capitation, verify the service is inside your capitation scope; if it is, the denial is correct and the revenue is the capitation payment itself
  4. If the assignment is wrong (patient not assigned to that group on that date), appeal with the eligibility evidence
  5. Update your routing rules so this plan's claims split correctly at intake
Corrected claim or appeal? Neither, in most cases — reroute the claim to the delegated group or carve-out vendor, or confirm the capitation covers it. Appeal only on a wrong assignment determination.

The deadline angle

Every remedy above runs against a clock. Corrected claims and appeals have to land inside the payer's filing and appeal windows, and those windows are counted from the date of service — see the standard timely filing limits by payer and verify against your contract. CO-24 — frequently asked questions

Can CO-24 be appealed?

Sometimes. If the patient was capitated to your group for that service category and date, no — the capitation payment is the payment. If the assignment or carve-out determination is wrong (wrong PCP assignment, service outside the capitation scope), appeal with the eligibility and assignment evidence.

How do I know if a service is really capitated?

Pull the eligibility and assignment record for the date of service: the plan's eligibility response shows the delegated group and capitation arrangement. Your contract's division-of-financial-responsibility (DOFR) document defines which services are inside the capitation.

How do we stop billing the plan for capitated services?

Routing logic at claim creation: eligibility responses identify the capitated arrangement, and the claim goes to the group or carve-out vendor instead of the plan. Repeated CO-24s are an intake/routing fix, not an appeals project.
