CO-256: Services not payable per the managed care contract

Group CO — Contractual Obligation · Denial Code Library · Updated October 29, 2026

What CO-256 means

Services are not payable under the managed care contract. Under the terms of the managed care arrangement, this payer does not pay this claim on a fee-for-service basis — the contract assigns the service to a different payment mechanism or entity.

What it usually means on a real remittance

CO-256 is a contract-interpretation denial. Like CO-24, it usually means the service is paid another way (capitation, case rate, delegated group) — but the referee here is the specific language of your managed care contract, so the first move is reading, not appealing.

Common causes

  • The service falls inside a category your managed care contract pays under a different mechanism (case rate, capitation, or a bundled arrangement)
  • The contract assigns this service to a risk arrangement where the payer is not the paying party
  • The claim reached the plan but belongs to the delegated entity under your contract's terms
  • The contract provision is being applied to a service it arguably doesn't cover — worth checking the exact contract language

How to fix or appeal CO-256

  1. Locate the contract provision the payer is applying and read it against the billed service
  2. If another entity or mechanism pays for this service under the contract, reroute the claim or the internal posting accordingly
  3. If the provision doesn't reach this service, appeal with the contract language quoted and the claim facts mapped to it
  4. Record the determination in your payer playbook so the same category routes correctly from intake
  5. Review the pattern quarterly: a rising CO-256 count after a contract renewal usually means the new terms changed routing and nobody updated the billing rules
Corrected claim or appeal? Contract first. Reroute if the contract assigns payment elsewhere; appeal with the contract text if the payer misapplied its own terms.

CO-256 — frequently asked questions

Where do I check whether CO-256 is right?

Read your managed care contract's payment provisions for the service category: which entity pays, under what mechanism, and whether this payer was correctly billed at all. CO-256 disputes are contract-reading exercises, not documentation exercises.

When is CO-256 appealable?

When the contract doesn't actually assign the service to another payment mechanism — payers do misapply their own contract terms. The appeal is the contract excerpt plus the claim facts, nothing else.

How is CO-256 different from CO-24?

They're siblings: CO-24 is the capitation/managed-care-plan version, CO-256 the broader managed-care-contract version. Both usually mean 'paid under a different arrangement — wrong door.'
