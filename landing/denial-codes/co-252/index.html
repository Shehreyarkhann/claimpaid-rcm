CO-252: Additional documentation is required to adjudicate this claim

Group CO — Contractual Obligation · Denial Code Library · Updated October 29, 2026

What CO-252 means

An attachment or other documentation is required to adjudicate this claim or service. The payer has paused the claim: it won't decide until the requested records, invoice, or other payer's remittance are supplied.

What it usually means on a real remittance

CO-252 is a suspended claim wearing a denial's clothes — nothing has been decided on the merits. The remark codes specify what's missing. The real cost is time: the claim ages while it waits, and other deadlines (appeal windows, filing limits on related claims) keep running.

Common causes

  • The payer needs the operative note, progress note, or test results to price or validate the service
  • An invoice is required (implants, DME, drugs billed with miscellaneous codes)
  • A primary payer's EOB is required and wasn't attached
  • The attachment was sent but not linked to the claim (wrong control number or document type at the clearinghouse)

How to fix or appeal CO-252

  1. Read the remark codes to identify the exact document requested — note, report, invoice, or primary EOB
  2. Send the document through the payer's specified channel with the claim identifiers on it, and log the transmission confirmation
  3. Confirm receipt after a few business days via claim status; unlinked attachments are common enough to verify, not assume
  4. Resubmit or notify per the payer's process once the attachment is linked (some payers reprocess automatically; others need a corrected claim or a call)
  5. Add the code to your attachment-required list so future claims carry the documentation from the start
Corrected claim or appeal? Neither appeal nor corrected claim in most cases — send the requested attachment through the payer's channel and verify it links to the claim.

The deadline angle

Every remedy above runs against a clock. Corrected claims and appeals have to land inside the payer's filing and appeal windows, and those windows are counted from the date of service — see the standard timely filing limits by payer and verify against your contract. CO-252 — frequently asked questions

How should I send the documentation?

Send exactly what the remark codes request, through the channel the payer specifies (portal upload, fax with the payer's coversheet, or the clearinghouse attachment transaction), and reference the claim number on every page. Documentation sent into a void is the top cause of repeat CO-252s.

Is CO-252 a real denial?

Nothing was decided about the service itself — the claim is incomplete, not denied on the merits. Treating CO-252 as a paperwork task (not an appeal) gets it paid fastest.

How do I prevent CO-252?

Attachment rules in the scrubber for codes that always require documentation (miscellaneous DME and drug codes, high-cost implants), so the attachment is assembled with the claim instead of after the denial. ClaimPaid's intake and claim workflows keep documents attached to the claim record.
