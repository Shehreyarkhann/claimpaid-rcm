What Is Cigna's Timely Filing Limit?

Standard published limits · Updated November 6, 2026 · Your payer contract controls — verify before acting

Cigna publishes a timely filing limit of 90 days from the date of service for participating providers, and 180 days for out-of-network providers. Many participating contracts extend the in-network window to 180 days — which is why 180 is the figure most billing teams know — but the published floor is 90, and the number in your agreement is the one that controls. Miss the window and the claim denies as CO-29.

How Cigna counts the clock

  • Three different clocks. Initial claims count from the date of service, corrected claims from the remittance, appeals from the denial notice. Using the wrong start date is the most common Cigna deadline error.
  • An unrequested resubmission gets no new clock. If Cigna didn't ask for the resubmission and it isn't an appeal, the normal filing limit still applies.
  • Consecutive-day services count from the last date of service, not the first.

If you miss Cigna's deadline

A Cigna CO-29 turns on proof and on which clock applied. If the claim was a correction, check whether the remittance-based corrected-claim window was still open and whether the original ERA was attached — corrections denied under the date-of-service clock by mistake are worth a reconsideration. A genuinely late original claim needs the clearinghouse acceptance record to survive.

The disclaimer that matters: Deadlines shown are the standard published timely-filing limits. Your contract with the payer controls the actual deadline for your claims — verify before acting on any specific claim.

Questions

What is Cigna's timely filing limit?

90 days from the date of service for participating providers and 180 days for out-of-network providers, per Cigna's published guidance. Many participating contracts extend the in-network window to 180 days — your agreement controls.

What is Cigna's corrected claim deadline?

Corrected claims are measured from the original remittance date: commonly 90 days in-network and 180 days out-of-network. Attach the original ERA so the correction is measured on the remittance clock.

How long do you have to appeal a Cigna denial?

180 calendar days from the initial payment or denial notice; for adjusted payments, 180 days from the last payment adjustment.

What happens if you miss Cigna's filing deadline?

The claim denies as CO-29 (time limit for filing expired). It is overturned only with proof of timely submission, or — for corrections — by showing the remittance-based corrected-claim window was still open.
