Cookie Policy

How ClaimPaid uses cookies

Last updated October 29, 2026 · Consent policy version 1

Cookies are small pieces of storage a website places on your device. This page lists — plainly and completely — every cookie and similar technology ClaimPaid uses, what it does, and how to control it. The short version: we run no advertising trackers, we do not sell personal information, and the only optional technology on this website is cookieless analytics that loads only if you allow it.

Your choices

When you first visit, a banner offers three equal choices: Accept all, Reject non-essential, or Customize. Rejecting is one click, just like accepting — we do not use dark patterns. Your choice is stored for 12 months. You can change it at any time:

The same control lives in the footer of every page on this site, under "Cookie settings". If we ever change what we use in a way that matters, the policy version goes up and every visitor is asked again.

What we store

NameCategoryPurposeExpires
cp_consent (cookie) Strictly necessary Remembers the cookie choices you made on this website — which categories you allowed, when, and under which policy version — so we can honor them and not ask on every page. 12 months
pulse_token (browser local storage, signed-in app only) Strictly necessary Your sign-in token for the ClaimPaid app. It keeps you logged in at /app/ and /ai/app/. This website reads it for one purpose only: if you are already signed in, it sends you straight to the app instead of showing you the marketing page. Until sign-out or token expiry

Analytics — only with your permission on this website

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which pages visitors read and how fast they load. It works through a small script (a "beacon") and is cookieless: it sets no cookies, builds no profile of you, and does not follow you across other sites.

On this website (the marketing and blog pages), the beacon does not load at all unless you grant the Analytics category. Rejecting costs you nothing — every page works the same.

Inside the signed-in ClaimPaid apps (/app/ and /ai/app/), the same cookieless measurement runs as product telemetry so we can keep the tools our customers work in fast and reliable. It is not used for advertising, and it never contains claim or patient data.

Marketing and functional cookies

None. ClaimPaid runs no advertising pixels, no remarketing tags, and no cross-site trackers, and no optional preference cookies beyond what is strictly necessary. Both categories appear in the settings panel so the structure is honest — and if either is ever introduced, it will arrive switched off, behind a fresh choice from you.

Other third-party requests

Two services deliver ordinary page content, the way a CDN would. Loading them tells those services your IP address, as any web request does:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site (Fraunces, Playfair Display, Inter) are served by Google.
  • Picsum (picsum.photos) — serves the header images on blog articles.

Neither is used by us to identify or track you.

We do not sell personal information

ClaimPaid does not sell personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is nothing to opt out of because the practice does not exist here. Details of what we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy.

Browser controls

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you delete the cp_consent cookie, this site will simply ask for your choice again on your next visit. Blocking strictly-necessary storage will keep you from staying signed in to the app.

Questions? Email support@claimpaid.io and we will answer plainly. This policy sits alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
