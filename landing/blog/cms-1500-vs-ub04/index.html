CMS-1500 vs UB-04: Which Claim Form to Use

ClaimPaid · October 24, 2026 · 7 min read
The short answer

Use the CMS-1500 (Form 1500) for professional and physician services, and the UB-04 (CMS-1450) for institutional and facility services — hospitals, SNFs, ASCs, home health. Electronically, they're the 837P and 837I. The wrong form gets rejected or misprocessed, so knowing which setting you're billing for is the whole decision.

Two claim forms run American healthcare billing, and mixing them up is a classic rookie error with expensive consequences. The rule is about setting, not specialty: physician work goes on one form, facility work on the other — even when the same organization does both.

Where the confusion happens

  • Ambulatory surgery centers bill the facility fee on UB-04 and the surgeon's professional fee on CMS-1500 — two claims, two forms, one encounter.
  • Hospital-employed physicians bill professional services on CMS-1500 even though the hospital bills the facility portion on UB-04.
  • Place of service vs form. POS codes (11 office, 22 outpatient hospital) go on the CMS-1500 to say where the service happened; the form choice says whose service it was — professional or facility.
  • DME and suppliers use the CMS-1500, not the UB-04 — supplier billing is professional-form territory.

What happens when you pick wrong

The wrong form typically fails at the payer's front end or the clearinghouse's edits — a rejection, not a denial — because the payer's system can't map the form to the provider type. The claim never adjudicates, and the timely-filing clock keeps running. If your rejections cluster around form-type errors, check whether new staff are defaulting to the wrong form for the setting.

Physician services go on the CMS-1500; facility services go on the UB-04. The wrong form gets rejected.

Getting it right systematically

  • Default by setting in your PM system. Facility locations should default to UB-04/837I workflows; clinic locations to CMS-1500/837P. Don't rely on memory per claim.
  • Scrub for form-setting mismatches. Your claim scrubber should flag POS codes that don't match the form type before submission.
  • Train on the edge cases. ASC split billing, hospital-based clinics, and provider-based departments are where experienced billers still get tripped up — document your organization's rules for each.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CMS-1500 and UB-04?

The CMS-1500 (Form 1500) is the professional claim form for physician and practitioner services, submitted electronically as the 837P. The UB-04 (CMS-1450) is the institutional claim form for facility services like hospitals and SNFs, submitted as the 837I.

Who uses the UB-04 claim form?

Hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, home health agencies, hospices, and ambulatory surgery centers use the UB-04 for their facility/institutional charges.

Do ambulatory surgery centers use CMS-1500 or UB-04?

Both: the facility fee goes on the UB-04 and the surgeon's professional fee goes on the CMS-1500 — two claims, two forms, for one encounter.

What happens if I submit on the wrong claim form?

The claim is typically rejected at the payer's front end (not denied), because the payer can't map the form to the provider type. It never adjudicates, and the timely-filing clock keeps running.

What is the electronic version of the CMS-1500?

The 837P (professional) is the electronic equivalent of the CMS-1500; the 837I (institutional) is the electronic equivalent of the UB-04.
