See what's about to expire.

Every payer gives you a fixed number of days from the date of service to file a claim. Miss it and the claim denies as CO-29 — usually for good. This audit ranks your open claims by days left against each payer's standard timely filing limit, so you can work the ones that are about to die first.

🔒
Your file never leaves your browser. The audit runs 100% on this device — your CSV is read locally and nothing about your claims is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere. The optional email form at the end sends only summary totals (counts and dollars), never claim rows.

How the deadlines are figured: deadline = date of service + the payer's standard published timely filing limit (Aetna 120 days, Cigna 180, UnitedHealthcare 90, Anthem 180, Medicare 365, NY Medicaid 90, Humana 180; other payers default to 365). Deadlines shown are the standard published timely-filing limits. Your contract with each payer controls the actual deadline for your claims — verify before acting on any specific claim.
